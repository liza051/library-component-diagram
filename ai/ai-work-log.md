# AI Work Log

## 1. Purpose of AI Assistance

AI was used as a supporting tool during the development of the Library Reservation System Component Diagram.

The main purpose of AI assistance was to support requirements analysis, identify possible gaps, ask clarifying questions, and suggest alternative solutions.

## 2. AI-Assisted Analysis

AI was used to:
- analyze the laboratory assignment requirements;
- identify potentially missing components and interfaces;
- point out unclear dependencies and architectural relationships;
- ask questions to clarify component responsibilities;
- suggest possible ways to represent external integrations;
- review the consistency of the architecture with previous laboratory models;
- identify potential discrepancies and suggest ways to resolve them.

## 3. Student's Work and Decisions

The student independently:
- analyzed the assignment requirements;
- determined the architecture structure;
- selected and described the system components;
- created and edited the component diagram;
- defined the system boundary, interfaces, and dependencies;
- compared the architecture with previous laboratory models;
- prepared the audit and project documentation;
- evaluated AI suggestions and selected the appropriate solutions.

All final decisions were made by the student.

## 4. Role of AI

AI did not act as the source of system requirements or make final architectural decisions.

Its role was limited to analytical support: identifying gaps, asking clarifying questions, and proposing possible solutions.

The student reviewed the suggestions, decided which ones were appropriate, and remained responsible for the final result.

## 5. Examples of AI-Assisted Work

During this laboratory work, AI assistance was used for the following tasks:

1. Reviewing the component diagram structure and checking whether the main components and interfaces were represented.
2. Comparing the component specification with the architecture diagram and identifying missing or unclear relationships.
3. Reviewing the audit and identifying discrepancies between the component model and previous laboratory models.
4. Improving the documentation to clarify the responsibilities of the Notification Service and the external Email Provider.

The student reviewed the suggestions and decided which changes should be included in the final model.
